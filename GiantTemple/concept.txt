The Giant Temple Automaton (Puppeteering / Boss Fights)In the center of the ruins is a dormant stone guardian or crane that you are trying to pilot using rope pulls and lever linkages.The Core Action: One lever controls the shoulder joint ($\alpha$), another controls the elbow joint ($\beta$). The hand needs to grab moving stone blocks, catch falling weights, or block incoming water/lava flows.The Identity Trick: This is raw angle addition: $\sin(\alpha + \beta)$ and $\cos(\alpha + \beta)$.When both arm segments align, the reach is huge; when they bend against each other, the arm folds up.To make the arm sweep smoothly along a flat ceiling or a flat wall without smashing into it, the player has to coordinate the two joints so the combined height or reach stays constant.Why a 10-Year-Old Plays: Piloting a giant stone mech or mechanical beast. The stakes feel immediate because the character is reacting to physical hazards in the environment, not sitting at a desk.
